The right instrument, one standard

Every job we scan is scanned with a Trimble X9, a survey-grade terrestrial laser scanner that levels and calibrates itself at each set-up. The manufacturer specifies 2 mm range accuracy and 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m. Larger areas are mapped by drone, fixed to ground control measured with an RTK GNSS rover, and checked against independent check points; the checked accuracy goes on every drone survey.

Every report states the registration error for that job, which is how well the individual scans tie together. If the data isn't tight enough for the check you need, we tell you rather than hand over a number we wouldn't stand behind. The same check applies when you send us your own survey data to report on. Questions we get asked

Which service do I need?

Before work starts, a Dimensure Design Audit checks the groundworks drawings. If you're building a road, a road and sub-base scan, and add a tarmac depth survey if thickness matters. If you're building houses, plot and oversite surveys. At handover, an as-built survey. For a warehouse slab or a screed, a floor flatness survey. For whole-site mapping, earthworks or stockpile quantities and monthly progress, a drone survey. For pegs on the ground, GNSS setting out, and for drainage and ducts before backfill, an as-laid survey. Before you start next to roads or houses, a pre-start condition survey. For planning or designing on a plot, a topographical survey. If you're designing or building something in an existing home or garden, a measured building survey or a garden survey.

Can you combine services in one visit?

Yes, and it's cheaper. Roads and plots on the same site are often scanned on the same day, and a house and garden can be surveyed together for an extension with landscaping.

Do you work for homeowners as well as contractors?

Yes. Road and plot scanning is mostly for contractors and housebuilders. Measured building, topographical and garden surveys are booked by homeowners, designers, architects and builders alike.
