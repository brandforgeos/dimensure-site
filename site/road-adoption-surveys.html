Road adoption surveys

Road adoption surveys: find the defects before the council does

Ponding, flat spots, low kerbs and proud ironwork are what hold up adoption. We scan the estate roads, check them against the section 38 drawings and specification, and hand you a located defect list while there's still time to fix it on your programme.

Get an adoption survey priceRoad adoption survey prices

In short: a road adoption survey laser scans finished estate roads and checks them against the approved section 38 drawings and specification: levels, crossfalls, longitudinal falls, kerb upstands, gully and ironwork levels and likely ponding. You get a defect schedule with every item located on plan, so it can be fixed before the highway authority's inspection. From £595.

What we check

  • Carriageway and footway levels against the design.
  • Crossfall and longitudinal fall, with flat areas flagged where water won't run.
  • Ponding: low spots in the surface model that will hold water, sized and located.
  • Kerb upstands along every run, including dropped kerbs and crossings.
  • Gullies and ironwork: covers and gratings proud of or below the surface, and gullies not at the low point.
  • Optional surface water flow model: where water will actually run, and which gullies it will reach, as a 3D model you can share.

Why do it before the inspection

The adoption engineer walks the roads looking for exactly these defects, usually after the maintenance period, when the site team has moved on. Every item found then has to be fixed and re-inspected, adoption slips, and the bond stays in place. A measured survey before the inspection turns that into a planned list of small repairs on your timetable.

It's also a dated record. If a defect appears later because of other trades or construction traffic, you have evidence of how the road was finished.

Common adoption snags we find

  • Ponding at turning heads and parking bays
  • Flat spots where crossfall changes
  • Kerb upstands too low or too high
  • Gully gratings sitting proud
  • Gullies not at the low point
  • Covers out of level with the surface

Built on how we already check roads

Road QC is where Dimensure started. We check sub-base and binder levels before each layer goes on, measure tarmac thickness from before-and-after scans, and record finished phases in as-built surveys. A road adoption survey brings that together at the end: one check of the finished road against what the authority will expect. If you want to see where surface water will and won't reach the gullies, we can add a flow model built from the scan.

If you'd rather catch these problems on the drawings before the road is built, a Dimensure Design Audit checks falls, gully positions and levels on paper first.

Working to your authority's standards

Every highway authority has its own design guide and specification. Send us the section 38 drawings, the specification and any tolerances in the agreement, and we report against those, not a generic standard.

Questions we get asked

What is a section 38 agreement?

An agreement under Section 38 of the Highways Act 1980 between a developer and the local highway authority, under which new estate roads are built to the authority's standards and then adopted, becoming publicly maintained highway. Adoption normally follows a maintenance period after the roads are finished, and a final inspection by the authority's engineer.

What does a pre-adoption survey check?

That the roads as built match the approved drawings and specification closely enough to pass inspection: carriageway and footway levels, crossfall and longitudinal fall, kerb upstands, gully and ironwork levels, and where water is likely to stand. Defects are listed and located so they can be put right before the authority's engineer finds them.

Why would we scan before the adoption engineer inspects?

To find the problems first. Ponding, flat spots, low kerbs and gullies sitting proud are much cheaper to fix on your programme than after a failed inspection, when the maintenance period can be extended and bonds held for longer.

Can you check for ponding without waiting for rain?

Yes. The scan gives a full surface model of the road, and low spots that will hold water show up in the analysis. As an optional extra, we can also model how surface water runs to the gullies, which shows where a gully is missing, cut off by levels or in the wrong place.

Do you survey drainage for section 104 adoption too?

We record manhole cover levels and positions, and as-laid drainage in open trenches before backfill. See as-laid service surveys. CCTV of sewers is a separate specialist service.

We've already got an as-built survey. Is this different?

An as-built survey records what's there. A pre-adoption survey goes further and checks what's there against what the authority will expect, with a pass, fail or watch on each item. We can do both from the same scan.
