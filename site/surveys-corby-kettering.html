Laser scan surveys in Corby and Kettering

We're based in Corby, so North Northamptonshire is home ground. Road and plot checks on the area's housing sites, and measured surveys for homes, kitchens and gardens, with no travel charge.

Covering: Corby, Kettering, Wellingborough, Rushden, Desborough, Rothwell, Thrapston, Oundle and the villages in between. Every Dimensure service is available, with no travel charge and short notice where the diary allows.

Housebuilding on our doorstep

North Northamptonshire has had some of the region's biggest housing schemes for years, from Priors Hall Park on the edge of Corby to Hanwood Park on the east side of Kettering, alongside a steady run of smaller sites in Wellingborough, Rushden and the market towns. Big schemes mean long programmes of estate roads and hundreds of plots reaching oversite, which is exactly where scanning earns its keep.

Being local makes a practical difference. A ten-minute drive means we can fit a plot check around a pour, come back to rescan a corrected oversite on the same day, or scan a sub-base first thing so the binder crew isn't kept waiting.

Homes, kitchens and gardens

The area's housing stock runs from Corby's 1950s and 60s estates and Kettering's Victorian terraces to brand-new homes. Each brings its own surveying headaches: walls that aren't square, floors that step between rooms, gardens built on slopes. A measured building survey or garden scan captures what's really there before anything is designed or ordered.

Plots, warehouses and handovers

Building a home on an infill plot in Corby, Kettering or the villages between? A topographical survey gives your architect the levels, boundaries and neighbouring buildings for the planning drawings. The warehouses along the A14 corridor need floors that racking will sit on, and a floor flatness survey checks the slab before it is covered. When a phase of road or plots is finished, an as-built survey records it for handover.

Drone, volume and setting-out work from Corby

We’re based in Corby, so the big sites around Priors Hall, Stanion and Kettering’s eastern edge are close enough for short-notice visits. That suits the work that has to happen when the site is ready: GNSS setting out of drainage and roads, as-laid records of pipes before backfill, and stockpile and earthworks volume surveys ahead of month-end valuations. Larger sites can have a monthly drone progress record, and before work starts next to existing streets, a pre-start condition survey records the roads and neighbouring homes. For homeowners, a drone roof survey gets a close look at a roof without scaffold.

Questions we get asked

Is there a travel charge in Corby or Kettering?

No. Corby is where we're based, and Kettering, Wellingborough, Rushden, Desborough, Rothwell, Thrapston and Oundle are all well within our standard travel radius.

How quickly can you get to a site in North Northamptonshire?

Often within a day or two, and sometimes the same day for a short plot or road check if the scanner is free. Booking a regular visit means you never wait.

Do you scan homes and gardens in Corby and Kettering too?

Yes. Measured building surveys for kitchens and extensions, and garden scans for landscape designs, are booked by homeowners and designers across the area.
