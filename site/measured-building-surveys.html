Who measured surveys are for

Kitchen and bathroom designers

Walls are rarely square and floors are rarely level. A scan shows both before you order, along with the exact position of the window, soil stack, boiler flue and every socket. Designs that fit first time mean fewer filler panels and fewer awkward conversations at installation.

Extensions, loft and garage conversions

Your architect or designer needs accurate existing drawings for planning and building regulations. We scan inside and out in one visit: rooms, the elevations being extended, eaves and ridge heights, and the ground around the house.

Interior designers and joiners

Built-in furniture, staircases and alcove units are made to measure. A point cloud gives you the real shape of the space, including the bow in the wall that a tape would miss.

Builders pricing a job

Price from real quantities instead of a quick tape-round. Floor areas, wall areas and openings are all in the model.

What you get

  • Scaled floor plans showing walls, openings, door swings, stairs, chimney breasts and fixed services.
  • Elevations of the walls you need, internal or external, with window and door heights and cill levels.
  • Room heights and floor levels between rooms, so steps and thresholds are known.
  • A registered point cloud in E57, RCP or LAS to open in AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp or kitchen design software.
  • On request: a 360° walkthrough from the scan positions for clients and trades.

Drawings are supplied as PDF and DWG. If your software needs a particular format or layer set-up, tell us when you book.

Scanning the outside for an extension

For a rear or side extension we scan the elevation being built against, the eaves and gutter line, rainwater and soil pipes, manholes, the boundary, and the ground levels across the area being built on. That's the information a designer needs to get the floor level, roof pitch and drainage right first time.

If the ground is being landscaped at the same time, the same visit can include a full garden survey. If your architect needs the whole plot for a planning application, see topographical surveys.

Already have a point cloud of the building? If the building has already been scanned, by your own scanner or another surveyor, we can draw the plans, elevations and sections from that point cloud instead of visiting. See reporting from your survey data.

Questions we get asked

What is a 3D measured building survey?

A survey that records the exact size and shape of a building, inside or out. We laser scan every room or elevation, join the scans into one 3D point cloud and draw the plans and elevations from it, so every wall, opening, step and out-of-square corner is where it really is.

Why scan instead of using a tape and laser measure?

Hand measurements miss things: walls that aren't square, floors that aren't level, a chimney breast that leans. A scan captures all of it in one visit, and if a designer needs a dimension later that nobody thought to take, it's already in the point cloud.

What do I get from a 3D measured building survey?

A registered point cloud (E57, RCP or LAS) and scaled floor plans and elevations as PDF and DWG. We can also provide a 360° walkthrough from the scan positions. Tell us what your designer or architect works in and we match it.

How long does a 3D measured building survey take?

Most rooms and whole houses are surveyed in a single visit, at a time that suits you. Drawings follow in three to five working days.

Is a laser scan survey accurate enough for a kitchen design or worktop template?

It is accurate enough to design from, with walls and openings to within a few millimetres. Stone and quartz worktops are normally still templated by the fabricator after the units are fitted, and we'd always recommend that.

Can a measured building survey cover the outside of a house for an extension?

Yes. We scan the rear or side elevations, eaves, rainwater goods, drains and the ground levels around the house, which gives your architect accurate existing drawings for planning and building regulations.

What is the difference between a measured building survey and a RICS building survey?

A measured building survey records the size and shape of a building; a RICS building survey reports on its condition. The measured survey gives plans, elevations and a point cloud that a designer works from. A RICS Level 2 or Level 3 survey is a surveyor's assessment of defects, usually before you buy. They answer different questions, and one does not replace the other.

How accurate is a 3D laser scan measured building survey?

Walls, openings and levels are captured to within a few millimetres. Our Trimble X9 is specified at 2 mm range accuracy and 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m, and inside a house most surfaces are much closer than that. The point cloud also records what a tape misses: walls that bow, rooms out of square and floors that step between rooms.

Can you produce a Revit or BIM model from a measured building survey?

Our standard deliverables are scaled 2D plans and elevations in PDF and DWG, plus the registered point cloud. The point cloud is supplied in RCP and E57, which link straight into Revit, so your team can model directly from it. If you need a Revit or IFC model produced for you, tell us when you enquire and we will confirm whether it can be quoted.

Do I need a measured survey for a planning application or building regulations?

Neither strictly requires one, but both need accurate drawings of the existing building. Planning applications include existing plans and elevations, and building regulations drawings depend on real floor levels, eaves heights and wall positions. Most architects either measure the house themselves or ask for a measured survey; a laser scan gives them both inside and out in one visit.

Do I need to empty the rooms before a measured building survey?

No. Furnished rooms are fine for kitchens, extensions and most floor plans, because the scanner captures the walls, openings and ceilings around the furniture. It can only measure what it can see, so if you need every wall surface, such as for fitted joinery, a tidy or empty room gives a more complete result. Moving clutter away from walls and windows helps.

Can you draw plans from a point cloud we already have?

Yes. Send the registered point cloud in E57, RCP, LAS or a similar format and tell us which plans, elevations or sections you need, and we draw them to the same standard as our own surveys. We check the scan coverage first, because rooms or faces the scanner never saw can't be drawn accurately.
