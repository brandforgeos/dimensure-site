Dimensure QC

Every plot. Every stage. On record.

The quality control app for groundworks on housebuilding sites. Stage sheets, photos, NHBC scores, defects and variations, signed off on the phone and kept as one complete record from foundation dig to CML.

Designed on site, not in an office. Built by a groundworks project manager with twelve years on housebuilding sites.

Teams · Site managers · Contracts managers · QS · Clients

One app, three jobs

Paper sheets get lost. Photos sit on someone's phone. Dimensure QC keeps the lot together.

1

Inspect

Every stage of every plot has its own sheet: the checks, the measurements, before, during and completed photos, delivery tickets and drawing references.

2

Sign off and score

Sheets land in the site manager's sign-off list. NHBC stages carry a score, failures raise a defect, and nothing moves on until the right stage is done.

3

Prove it

When a plot closes, its whole history becomes a completion certificate: every answer, every photo and every signature, start to finish.

Stage sheets

Fourteen stages, from foundation dig to handover

Each stage has a sheet built for that job, so the right questions get asked every time on every plot.

  • Yes, No or N/A on every check, with measurements and notes where they matter.
  • Before, during and completed photos, three at a time, straight from the camera.
  • Concrete and material delivery tickets photographed on the sheet.
  • Drawing references and revisions cited, so you know what it was built to.
  • Signed on the screen by the team and the site manager.
Site manager

Your day on one screen

Open the app and the work is waiting: sheets to sign off, overdue defects, variations at risk and advisories for your house types.

  • Sheets to sign off listed by plot, stage and who filled them in.
  • NHBC stage scores recorded on the sheet, with failures turned into defects.
  • Stage order enforced, with a reasoned override when the site needs one.
  • Every plot's progress at a glance, from first dig to CML.
Defects

Defects found, fixed and closed, with the proof

A defect raised on a sheet stays open until a fix sheet closes it, with photos and a sign-off.

  • Open, overdue and due-soon defects up front, by plot and stage.
  • Cause recorded: workmanship, design or materials. Only workmanship counts against a team's score.
  • Fix attempts logged with the remedial work and a re-score.
  • Technical advisories pushed to every plot of the same house type.
Variations

Every variation tracked from site to order

Raised on site with photos and drawings, priced by the QS, sent to the principal contractor and chased until the order comes back.

  • Secured and not-yet-ordered values at the top of the register.
  • Commercial risk flagged when work is done without an order.
  • Hold-ups listed, so everyone knows what's stopping it going to the PC.
  • Values seen only by the QS, Contracts Manager and Admin.

Questions about Dimensure QC

Does Dimensure QC need downloading from an app store?

No. It runs in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to the home screen so it opens like an app. Nothing to install, nothing to update.

Do the gangs need training?

Very little. A team login sees its plots, taps a plot, then a stage. Buttons are big enough to use in gloves and the wording is site English, not software English.

Who can see prices and variation values?

Only the Quantity Surveyor, Contracts Manager and Admin logins. Site managers, teams and view-only client logins never see money. The check happens on the server, not just on the screen.

Can a signed-off sheet be changed?

Not silently. A completed sheet can be amended, but the amendment sits alongside the original with who changed it, when, why and exactly what changed. The original is never overwritten.

Can we print the record for a plot?

Yes. Any plot's record can be opened as a PDF from the plot screen: every stage sheet, every answer, every photo, defects, variations and the full history. On a closed plot it becomes the completion certificate.

Can we try it before talking to anyone?

Yes. The live demo runs on sample data with a bar at the top to switch between team, site manager, contracts manager, QS, client and admin logins. Nothing you do there touches real data.
