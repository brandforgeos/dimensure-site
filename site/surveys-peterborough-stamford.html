Laser scan surveys in Peterborough and Stamford

Covering: Peterborough and its townships, Stamford, Bourne, Market Deeping, Oundle and Yaxley. Every Dimensure service is available, all inside our standard travel radius.

Peterborough: a city that keeps growing

Peterborough has grown through planned townships for decades, from Hampton in the south to Paston Reserve in the north, and new phases keep coming. For groundworkers and housebuilders that means a constant flow of estate roads and plots to check. We scan sub-base and binder against design, map tarmac thickness for adoption, and check plots at oversite in batches to keep the per-plot cost low.

Stamford: stone, history and careful design

Stamford's limestone buildings are some of the most distinctive in England, and almost none of them are square. Whether it's an extension, a new kitchen in a period house or a design for a walled garden, a laser scan survey records every irregular wall, deep reveal and uneven floor exactly. Because the scanner never touches the building, it suits listed and conservation-area properties well, and the garden scan does the same for stone walls, steps and terraces outside.

Surveys for plots, phases and floors

Infill plots and barn conversions around Stamford and the villages often need a topographical survey that captures stone walls, mature trees and the neighbouring buildings. Peterborough's housing and industrial growth means finished phases to record with an as-built survey, and new unit floors to check with a floor flatness survey.

Questions we get asked

Do you cover Peterborough's new townships?

Yes. Hampton, Paston and the newer growth areas to the south and east of the city are all covered, along with the older city and its villages.

Can you survey listed or stone buildings in Stamford?

Yes. Laser scanning is ideal for historic buildings because it measures without touching anything and captures irregular stonework exactly as it is. Your conservation architect gets plans, elevations and the full point cloud.

Is there a travel charge?

No. Peterborough, Stamford, Bourne and the surrounding area are all within our standard 50-mile travel radius from Corby.
