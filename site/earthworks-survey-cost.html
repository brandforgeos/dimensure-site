What drives the price

Site size and shape

Larger sites take longer to control and fly, and long, narrow sites need more control along their length for the same accuracy.

What the comparison is against

A 3D design surface from your engineer goes straight into the comparison. If the design is only on 2D levels drawings, we build the surface first, which takes office time.

How the volumes are split

One total for the site is quick. Splitting the quantities by roads, plots, open space or by phase, to match a bill of quantities, takes longer and is priced when we quote.

Ground the drone can't see

Hedges, trees and standing crops hide the ground. Those areas are levelled with the RTK GNSS rover so the volumes are complete.

How our prices compare

Published UK guides in 2026 put drone mapping of a 10-hectare development site at £1,500–£4,000 (Dronedesk's UK drone survey price guide, May 2026), and volume analysis is often an extra. Our prices are for the finished quantities, comparison included.

Questions we get asked

How much does an earthworks volume survey cost?

With Dimensure, an earthworks survey of a site up to about two hectares (5 acres), compared with the design or a previous survey, costs from £595. Each repeat survey on the same site costs from £395. Larger sites are priced individually. Prices are before VAT, with travel included within 50 miles of Corby.

What does a cut and fill survey cost in the UK?

Published UK guides in 2026 put drone mapping of a 10-hectare development site at around £1,500 to £4,000, with smaller sites less. Volume calculations against a design are often charged on top of the mapping. Our price includes the comparison, the heat-map and the volumes.

Is a pre-start survey worth paying for?

Almost always. It is the only record of the original ground, and every later volume, from the topsoil strip to the final muck-away figure, is measured against it. Once the ground has been moved it can't be recreated, so a few hundred pounds at the start can settle disputes worth thousands at the end.

What makes an earthworks survey cost more?

A large or long, thin site, heavy vegetation or standing crops that need levelling with the rover, a design that has to be built from 2D drawings rather than supplied as a 3D surface, many separate areas to report, and urgent turnaround.
