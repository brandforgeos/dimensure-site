What decides the price

Number of scanner set-ups

The scanner sees in straight lines, so a building of small rooms, stairs and corridors needs more set-ups than an open warehouse of the same floor area. Set-ups drive time on site, and time on site drives the price.

Inside, outside, or both

Scanning the inside and outside of a building, and joining them through doors and windows, takes longer than one or the other. If you only need the rear elevation for an extension, say so and you'll pay for that.

Colour

Colour images add time at every set-up. Plain intensity data is fine for most drawing work, so we only capture colour when it helps you.

Access

Occupied buildings, restricted hours and escorted sites take longer. A clear, empty building is the quickest and cheapest to scan.

Questions we get asked

How much does a point cloud survey cost?

With Dimensure, a point cloud survey costs from £395 for up to a half-day on site, which covers most houses, a small commercial unit or a typical garden. Larger buildings and sites are priced on the time they need. The price includes registration, cleaning and delivery in E57, RCP or LAS. Prices are before VAT, with travel included within 50 miles of Corby.

Why is a point cloud cheaper than a measured survey?

Because there's no drawing time. Most of the cost of a measured building survey is the office time to draw plans, elevations and sections. If you or your designer draw from the cloud yourselves, you only pay for the scanning and processing.

What is the typical day rate for laser scanning in the UK?

Published UK rates for laser scanning commonly sit around £750 to £1,250 a day for a registered point cloud. We price per job instead, so a small building that only needs a few hours isn't charged as a full day.

Does tying the point cloud to OS grid cost extra?

A little, because it means setting out and observing control with the GNSS rover while we're on site. On most jobs it adds less than an hour, and we include it in the fixed price when you ask for it at booking.
