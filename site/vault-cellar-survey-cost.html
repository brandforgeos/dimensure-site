Cost guide

Vault and cellar survey cost

What a non-intrusive scan of a vault, cellar or void costs, and what pushes the price up.

Guide price: a vault or cellar scan costs from £395 for up to a half-day on site, with the registered point cloud. An annotated plan and critical sections for the engineer are quoted per job. Before VAT. Travel is included within 50 miles of Corby and added for jobs further away.

JobGuide priceIncludes
Scan and point cloudfrom £395Up to a half-day on site from the existing openings, registered point cloud
Plan, sections and report for the engineerquotedAnnotated plan, critical sections, levels, images, measured vs inferred
Urgent attendancequotedWe'll tell you the earliest date when you ask
Outside 50 miles of Corbyplus travelShown separately on the quote

Guide prices before VAT. Every quote is fixed and itemised before we book. Geophysics and intrusive investigation are not included.

What decides the price

How much can be seen

A large opening into a clear vault is quick to scan. A small hole into a long, partly filled vault needs more set-ups and more care to get the scanner's view where it's needed.

What the engineer needs

A point cloud alone is the quickest output. Drawn plans and sections relating the vault to the neighbouring wall, boundary and piles take office time, so they're priced on what's needed.

Distance and urgency

Within 50 miles of Corby, travel is included. Further away, travel is added and shown on the quote.

Questions we get asked

How much does a vault or cellar survey cost?

With Dimensure, a non-intrusive vault or cellar scan starts from £395 for up to a half-day on site, with the registered point cloud. An annotated plan and critical sections for the structural engineer are quoted per job, because they depend on how much of the structure can be seen and what the engineer needs. Prices are before VAT; travel is included within 50 miles of Corby and added for jobs further away, such as London.

Can you give a fixed price before you've seen it?

Yes. Send a photo of the opening, a site plan or sketch and the engineer's questions, and we'll give a fixed price for the scan and the drawings. If the photo shows a scan can't answer the question, we'll tell you before you spend anything.

Is GPR included?

No. A laser scan measures what can be seen from the openings. If the buried extent needs a geophysical survey such as GPR, that's a separate specialist service, agreed with you before any cost is incurred.
