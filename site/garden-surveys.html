Garden surveys

Garden surveys: every level, laser scanned

Design the garden on real levels. We scan the whole plot, from the damp-proof course to the back fence, and give your designer or landscaper a scaled levels plan, a 3D model and the point cloud.

What a garden survey captures

  • Ground levels across the whole garden, with contours at your chosen interval.
  • The house: damp-proof course height, door thresholds, air bricks, downpipes and gullies.
  • Existing patios, paths, steps, walls and their heights.
  • Boundaries: fences, walls and hedges, with heights.
  • Trees with trunk position, spread and approximate height.
  • Manholes and inspection chambers, with cover levels.

Why it saves money on a garden build

Most garden builds that go over budget do it underground: more excavation than expected, a retaining wall that has to be taller, drainage that wasn't planned, or a patio that ends up too close to the damp-proof course. All of those come from levels nobody measured properly.

With a scan, the designer sets patio and lawn levels from real numbers, steps come out even, wall heights are known before pricing, and excavation and muck-away can be calculated rather than estimated. The landscaper prices what will really be built.

Front gardens and driveways

The same survey works for a front garden or driveway: falls to the road, the dropped kerb, manholes, the house's damp-proof course and the boundary walls. It is the information you need to design a drive that drains away from the house.

Questions we get asked

What is a garden scan?

A 3D laser survey of the whole garden. We capture the ground levels, the house wall and its damp-proof course, steps, walls, fences, trees, drains and manholes, then give you a scaled plan with levels and contours, plus the point cloud and a 3D model.

Why do I need levels for a garden design?

Levels decide almost everything in a garden build: how many steps, how high a retaining wall, where water drains, whether a patio sits 150 mm below the damp-proof course, and how much soil comes out. A design drawn without them is guesswork.

Is a garden scan the same as a topographical survey?

Yes. For a garden or small plot it is a topographical survey: ground levels, contours, boundaries, walls, trees, buildings and drainage covers on a scaled plan. It goes further than a traditional topo survey because you also get the full 3D scan, so anything missed on the plan can still be measured later. For larger plots, development sites and planning work, see our topographical surveys. For planning applications that need a survey on Ordnance Datum, tell us at booking and we'll tie the scan to it.

How long does a garden laser scan take?

Most gardens are scanned in a single visit, and you don't need to be in for all of it. The levels plan follows in three to five working days.

Does Dimensure design the garden as well as scanning it?

No, we measure. The scan goes to your garden designer or landscaper. If you need a builder for the work, we can pass you to contractors who use our surveys.

How accurate is a garden laser scan?

Far more accurate than a garden design needs: points are measured to millimetres, and the levels plan shows spot levels and contours at 100 mm or your chosen interval. Our Trimble X9 is specified at 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m. In practice the limit is what the scanner can see, so dense planting and clutter matter more than the instrument.

How far below the damp-proof course should a patio be?

Generally at least 150 mm below the damp-proof course, with the patio falling away from the house. That gap stops rain splashing above the DPC and water bridging into the wall; level thresholds need a drainage channel and careful detailing. A garden scan records the DPC height, door thresholds and air bricks, so the designer sets patio levels from real numbers instead of finding the problem on site.

Does a garden scan include a tree survey?

It records each tree's trunk position, spread and approximate height, which is what a garden designer needs for layout. It is not an arboricultural tree survey. If trees affect a planning application or foundations, a BS 5837 survey by an arboriculturist assesses condition, root protection areas and constraints. The scan gives them an accurate base plan to work on.

Can a garden scan find underground drains and services?

It finds what is visible: manholes, inspection chambers, gullies, downpipes and cover levels. A laser scanner cannot see buried pipes or cables, so drain runs between chambers are not shown. If you need those, a CCTV drain survey or utility trace is the right tool, and the results can be added to the scan plan by your designer.

Can a laser scanner measure the ground under dense planting?

Only where it can see it. The scanner needs a line of sight to the surface, so ground hidden under thick shrubs, hedges or long grass is not measured directly. We add set-ups to see behind walls and planting, which is why heavily planted gardens can take longer, and any area we could not capture is marked on the plan rather than guessed.

What software can a garden designer open a garden scan in?

The levels plan comes as PDF and DWG, which opens in AutoCAD, Vectorworks and most landscape design packages. The point cloud is supplied in standard formats such as E57 or LAS. A cleaned 3D surface model in DWG or a SketchUp-ready format can be added for designers who model the garden in 3D.

Can you make a garden levels plan from an existing survey?

Yes. Send the scan, drone survey or level data with the coordinate system it is in, and we produce a scaled levels plan with spot levels and contours for your designer. The plan can only show what the survey captured, so areas under dense planting or out of sight will be marked rather than guessed.
