The problem

The road levels looked right on their own. So did the drainage layout. But where kerb build-outs and pinch points sat across channels falling towards them, the nearest gully was on the downhill side, or missing. Water would hit the kerb face and stop.

What we found

We traced every channel from its high point to an outlet, with the kerb layout laid over the drainage. Four gullies were needed on the first sheet reviewed, and eighteen more across the rest of the site. All 22 were added to the drawings before construction.
