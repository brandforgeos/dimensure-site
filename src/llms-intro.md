# Dimensure

> Dimensure is a 3D laser scanning and construction quality-control survey company based in Corby, Northamptonshire, UK. It scans estate roads, house plots, buildings and gardens with a Trimble X9 survey-grade terrestrial laser scanner and reports whether what has been built matches the design.

Key facts:

- Services: Dimensure Design Audit (pre-construction review of groundworks and civils drawings for any project, including housing, commercial, industrial and infrastructure; used by designers and engineers, housebuilders, main contractors, and groundworks/civils contractors: levels, drainage, foundations, cross-drawing clashes; graded issue log with suggested fixes for the designer and indicative fix costs; from £495 per site); road and sub-base laser scanning against design levels; tarmac (asphalt) depth surveys by before-and-after scan comparison; plot and oversite scanning (slab flatness, brickwork dimensions to drawing, drain collar positions); 3D measured building surveys; garden and landscape scanning.
- Areas served: the whole of the Midlands as standard (travel included within 50 miles of Corby, e.g. Northampton, Leicester, Coventry, Nottingham, Peterborough, Milton Keynes, Bedford, Cambridge); anywhere else in the UK with travel charged.
- Quality: scans are processed by software, then every report is checked by the Dimensure team, who sense-check results against drawings and site and flag problems.
- Equipment: Trimble X9, manufacturer-specified 2 mm range accuracy and 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m; every report states the registration error for the job.
- Turnaround: road and plot QC reports normally the next working day; measured building and garden surveys 3-5 working days.
- Guide prices (before VAT): plot checks from £85 per plot (4+ plots per visit); road scans from £495 per visit; tarmac depth surveys from £895 per section; measured building surveys from £325; garden scans from £395.
- Scope note: plot checks do not verify plot position on the site grid or level to site datum; those remain with the site engineer.
- Set up by a groundworks project manager with 12 years on UK housing sites (NVQ Level 6 Construction Contracting Operations Management, SMSTS, CSCS Black card).
