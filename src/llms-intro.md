# Dimensure

> Dimensure is a laser scan, drone and GNSS survey company (measured building, topographical, as-built, floor flatness, drone mapping, volume, setting out and construction quality-control surveys) based in Corby, Northamptonshire, UK. It scans estate roads, house plots, buildings and gardens with a Trimble X9 survey-grade terrestrial laser scanner and reports whether what has been built matches the design, and maps larger areas by drone tied to RTK GNSS ground control. It can also process survey data supplied by a client or the client's own engineer (laser scans, drone surveys, total station or GPS levels) and report it against design without a site visit.

Key facts:

- Services: Dimensure Design Audit (pre-construction review of groundworks and civils drawings for any project, including housing, commercial, industrial and infrastructure; used by designers and engineers, housebuilders, main contractors, and groundworks/civils contractors: levels, drainage, foundations, cross-drawing clashes; graded issue log with suggested fixes for the designer and indicative fix costs; from £495 per site); road and sub-base laser scanning against design levels; tarmac (asphalt) depth surveys by before-and-after scan comparison; plot and oversite surveys (slab flatness, brickwork dimensions to drawing, drain collar positions); as-built surveys of finished roads, drainage covers, plots and external works with as-built drawings; floor flatness surveys of slabs and screeds against BS 8204 SR classes or the specified flatness class; measured building surveys (floor plans, elevations, sections); topographical surveys for planning and design; garden surveys; drone surveys (orthophoto, surface model, contours, point cloud, fixed to RTK GNSS ground control with checked accuracy, typically 30-50 mm in level on open hard ground); stockpile volume surveys by drone (m³ and tonnes per pile); earthworks volume surveys (cut and fill against design or a previous survey, topsoil strip, muck-away checks); construction progress drone surveys (monthly controlled orthophoto and fixed-viewpoint photos); RTK GNSS setting out and site control on Ordnance Survey National Grid (typically 10-20 mm plan, 20-30 mm level); as-laid surveys of drainage, chambers and ducts in open trench before backfill; pre-start and post-completion condition surveys of highways and neighbouring properties (laser scan, drone, referenced photos, defect schedule); drone roof surveys (inspection and measured roof report).
- Reporting from client survey data: Dimensure does not have to carry out the survey. Clients can send their own engineer's scans, drone surveys or levels and get the same QC report (heat-maps, pass/fail, thickness, flatness, measured drawings); no site visit, available anywhere in the UK, fixed price per job after seeing the data.
- Areas served: the whole of the Midlands as standard (travel included within 50 miles of Corby, e.g. Northampton, Leicester, Coventry, Nottingham, Peterborough, Milton Keynes, Bedford, Cambridge); anywhere else in the UK with travel charged.
- Quality: scans are processed by software, then every report is checked by the Dimensure team, who sense-check results against drawings and site and flag problems.
- Equipment: Trimble X9, manufacturer-specified 2 mm range accuracy and 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m; every report states the registration error for the job. Survey drone with a 1-inch sensor and an RTK GNSS rover; drone surveys state their accuracy checked against independent ground check points. Drone flights follow UK Civil Aviation Authority rules.
- Turnaround: road and plot QC reports normally the next working day; measured building and garden surveys 3-5 working days.
- Guide prices (before VAT): plot checks from £85 per plot (4+ plots per visit); road scans from £495 per visit; tarmac depth surveys from £895 per section; measured building surveys from £325; topographical surveys from £595; as-built surveys from £595 per visit; floor flatness surveys from £325 (floors up to 2,500 m² from £595); garden surveys from £395; drone surveys from £450 (up to ~1 ha) and £850 (up to ~5 ha); stockpile surveys from £395 per visit (from £295 on a regular programme); earthworks volume surveys from £595 (repeat surveys from £395); progress surveys from £295 per visit (single visit from £395); GNSS setting out from £325 per half day and £550 per day (site control from £395); as-laid surveys from £325 per visit; condition surveys from £395; drone roof surveys from £175 (with measured roof report from £295; commercial from £450).
- Scope note: plot checks do not verify plot position on the site grid or level to site datum; those remain with the site engineer.
