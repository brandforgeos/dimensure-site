Covering: Market Harborough, Kibworth, Lutterworth, Oadby, Wigston, Blaby and Leicester. Every Dimensure service is available. The whole county is within our standard travel radius, so travel is included.

Housing sites from Harborough to the city

Market Harborough sits a short drive from our base, and the town and its villages have taken a lot of new housing in recent years. Further north, large schemes such as New Lubbesthorpe and Ashton Green on the edge of Leicester bring long road programmes and steady plot completions.

For contractors on those sites we offer the same routine as everywhere else: road layers checked against design before they're covered, tarmac thickness mapped from before-and-after scans, and oversites checked plot by plot.

Leicester's homes, measured properly

Leicester has a huge stock of Victorian and Edwardian terraces and inter-war semis, the houses most often extended, knocked through and refitted. They are also the houses where hand measurements go wrong. A 3D measured survey records every out-of-square wall and stepped floor, and a garden scan does the same outside.

Topographical, as-built and floor flatness surveys

For plots and extensions across Leicestershire, a topographical survey gives the architect accurate levels and boundaries. South Leicestershire's logistics parks need flat, level floors, and a floor flatness survey checks them edge to edge. On housing sites, as-built surveys record finished roads, covers and plots at handover.

Drone and GNSS surveys in Leicestershire

From the housing sites around Market Harborough and Lutterworth to farmland and estates in the county, a drone survey maps large areas quickly and accurately with RTK GNSS ground control. Groundworkers use our earthworks and stockpile volume surveys for quantities, and developers book monthly progress flights. We also offer GNSS setting out, as-laid records of drainage before backfill, pre-start condition surveys for jobs next to existing homes and roads in the city, and drone roof surveys for houses, commercial buildings and farm roofs.
