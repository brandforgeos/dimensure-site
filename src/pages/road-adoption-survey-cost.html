What decides the price

Road length and layout

Straight roads need fewer scanner set-ups than turning heads, parking courts and shared surfaces, which have more kerbs, more falls and more places for water to sit.

The drawings you send

A 3D design surface or a DWG with levels goes straight into the comparison. Scanned PDFs mean the design has to be built first, which takes longer.

Parked cars and traffic

Cars parked over the kerbs and gullies hide exactly what we need to see. A visit when the road is as clear as possible gives a complete result in less time.
