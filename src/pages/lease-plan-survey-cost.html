What decides the price

How many demises

Most of the work is on site. Measuring a whole building of flats on one visit is far cheaper per flat than coming back for each one as it's let.

Size and layout

A single-room shop is quick; a three-floor maisonette with a garden, store and parking space takes longer to measure and draw.

Access

Occupied flats need appointments with each tenant. Empty buildings, or one day with every door open, keep the cost down.
