Oundle: stone buildings and conservation

Oundle is built almost entirely of local limestone, and much of the town centre sits in a conservation area with many listed buildings. Any alteration needs good existing drawings, and old stone buildings are hard to draw by hand. A listed building survey scans the building inside and out without touching it, and gives plans, elevations and sections that show the real wall thicknesses and roof lines.

Barns and farm buildings

The villages between Oundle, Thrapston and the A1 are full of stone barns and farm buildings, many coming up for conversion under Class Q or full planning. Our barn conversion surveys give the architect and engineer drawings of the building as it really stands. For the ground around it, a topographical survey records levels, access and boundaries.

Thrapston and the A14

Thrapston sits on the A14, with employment land and units around it. We provide floor flatness surveys for new unit floors, lease plans when they're let, and construction checks on new housing, from plot surveys to pre-adoption road surveys.

Gardens, paddocks and arenas

Country homes here come with big gardens and often a paddock or two. Garden surveys give designers the levels across a sloping plot, and arena level surveys show riding arena owners exactly where the surface has moved.
