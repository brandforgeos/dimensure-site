Covering: Desborough, Rothwell, Rushton, Braybrooke, Stoke Albany and the villages between Corby, Kettering and Market Harborough. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

Ironstone homes

Both towns have old centres built in local ironstone, with terraces and cottages that have been extended and altered many times. Their walls are thick and rarely straight. An extension or loft survey gives the architect the house as it is, and a kitchen survey means units and worktops fit walls that aren't square. Where a floor has dropped or a gable has started to lean, floor level and wall verticality surveys give the engineer the numbers.

New estates

New housing on the edges of both towns keeps groundworks teams busy. We scan plots at oversite, check road layers before they're covered and record finished phases in as-built surveys, with pre-adoption surveys at the end.

Villages, barns and gardens

In the villages, barn conversion surveys, garden surveys and topographical surveys are the usual starting points for a project.
