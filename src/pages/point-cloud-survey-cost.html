What decides the price

Number of scanner set-ups

The scanner sees in straight lines, so a building of small rooms, stairs and corridors needs more set-ups than an open warehouse of the same floor area. Set-ups drive time on site, and time on site drives the price.

Inside, outside, or both

Scanning the inside and outside of a building, and joining them through doors and windows, takes longer than one or the other. If you only need the rear elevation for an extension, say so and you'll pay for that.

Colour

Colour images add time at every set-up. Plain intensity data is fine for most drawing work, so we only capture colour when it helps you.

Access

Occupied buildings, restricted hours and escorted sites take longer. A clear, empty building is the quickest and cheapest to scan.
