What the survey shows

  • Surface levels and contours across the whole arena or pitch.
  • High and low spots compared with a best-fit plane or the design fall, coloured so they're obvious.
  • Falls: which way and how much the surface falls, and where it doesn't fall at all.
  • Track and corners: how much material has moved off the track and where it's ended up.
  • Surface depth, when the base is surveyed before the surface goes down, or at test holes.
  • Cut and fill to bring it back to level or fall, so you know roughly how much material moves.

Getting it right first time

An arena is only as good as its base. Before a new surface goes down, a survey of the sub-base checks the fall and level while it's still easy to correct; afterwards, a second survey proves the surface depth is even. On an existing arena, the survey tells you whether a regrade and top-up will do, or whether the problem is underneath.
