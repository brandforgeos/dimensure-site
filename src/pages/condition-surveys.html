What the condition record covers

  • Carriageway: surface, cracks, patches, potholes, ironwork and existing reinstatements.
  • Footpaths and kerbs: cracked flags or tarmac, chipped, sunk or displaced kerbs, dropped crossings.
  • Verges, trees and gullies: verge edges, tree pits, gully grates and covers.
  • Boundary walls and fences next to the site, with any lean or cracking measured.
  • Neighbouring buildings: the outside of adjoining properties from the road and site, and the inside with permission.
  • A written schedule of existing defects, each referenced to photos and a location.

Why a scan beats a folder of photos

Most pre-start records are a few hundred phone photos with no locations and no scale. They're better than nothing, but when a neighbour says their wall has started to lean, or the council says the road has been damaged by your wagons, comparing two photos taken from slightly different spots proves very little.

A laser scan records the actual shape of the road, kerbs and walls. Scanned again at the end, the two can be compared point by point, so a claimed rut, settlement or lean can be measured in millimetres. The drone adds a dated overhead view of the whole frontage, and every photo is tied to a location on the plan.

Protecting the job and the neighbours

A good record isn't only defensive. It shows neighbours and the council that the job is being run properly, makes genuine damage quick to identify and put right, and stops arguments about pre-existing cracks before they start.

Who books condition surveys

  • Groundworks and civils contractors before site access, drainage connections and road works.
  • Housebuilders with planning conditions on the adjoining highway.
  • Builders and developers working next to existing homes, especially with deep digs or demolition.
  • Homeowners with major works starting next door.

How a condition survey works

  1. Extent

    The site plan and which roads, frontages and properties to record. A planning condition helps if there is one.

  2. Fixed price

    We confirm the extent, whether interiors are included and the deliverables, and book before your start date.

  3. Record

    Laser scan, drone flight and referenced photos of everything in the agreed extent.

  4. Schedule

    Written schedule of defects, photo set, aerial image and scan, within three working days.

What a condition survey isn't

It's a factual record of visible condition on the day, not a structural survey, and it doesn't say what caused any defect. Where the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 applies, the appointed party wall surveyor deals with the neighbour's schedule under the Act; our record supports it. Inside neighbouring properties needs the owner's permission. Where a council wants a joint highway inspection, we can attend with their officer.
