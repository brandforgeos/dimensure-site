What decides the price

Data quality

A clean, well-registered scan with good coverage draws quickly. Noisy, patchy or poorly registered data takes longer, and sometimes can't be drawn reliably at all; we'll tell you before you pay.

What you need drawn

The number of floors, elevations and sections, and the level of detail on each, drive the drawing time.

Templates and standards

Drawing to your template is no problem, and a clear template actually saves time compared with guessing what you want.
