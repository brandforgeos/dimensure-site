Covering: Melton Mowbray, Asfordby, Bottesford, Waltham on the Wolds, Somerby, the Vale of Belvoir and north-east Leicestershire. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

Yards, arenas and equestrian centres

Melton has a long equestrian history, and the country around it is full of yards. An arena level survey shows exactly where a riding arena's surface has drifted off the track and into the corners, and how far it is out of fall, so a regrade is planned on numbers.

Barns and farm buildings

Stone and brick farm buildings across the Vale of Belvoir and the wolds are being converted to homes and offices. Our barn conversion surveys draw them as they stand, and a topographical survey covers the yard, access and boundaries around them.

The town

In Melton itself, older town-centre buildings need listed building surveys and lease plans when they're altered or split, and homes across the town need extension and loft surveys and kitchen surveys before work starts. Where floors or walls have moved, floor level and wall verticality surveys give the engineer measured evidence.

New housing

On the new estates around the town, we offer the full construction range: road level checks, plot surveys and pre-adoption road surveys.
