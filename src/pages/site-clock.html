Sign in & out

A selfie at the gate, not a queue at the cabin.

Each operative signs in on their own phone in seconds. No cards to lose, no signing in for a mate.

  • Face check every time. A quick selfie with a head turn is matched to the photo they took when they were set up.
  • Location checked. They have to be within the site's sign-in area, so nobody signs in from the van down the road.
  • Moving sites? They just sign in at the next one and their hours on the first site stop on their own.
  • No phone? The site manager signs them in with a face scan on their own phone.
  • No signal? The sign-in is kept on the phone with the real time and sends itself later.

RAMS, toolbox talks & tickets

Nobody starts until they've signed the RAMS.

Issue RAMS, toolbox talks or safety alerts to a site or the whole company. If it isn't signed, they can't sign in.

  • Once or every day. Choose whether a document is signed once or at every sign-in, like a daily briefing.
  • New revision, new signatures. Issue Rev C and everyone signs it again before their next sign-in.
  • See who's signed. A list of who has signed and who still needs to, for every document.
  • Tickets and cards. CSCS, plant tickets and training with expiry dates, checked by the site manager.

Fire roll call

Alert every phone on site. Know who's safe.

One red button starts the roll call from the list of everyone signed in, and sends an emergency alert to their phones.

  • Your message, your assembly point. Type the emergency message and where to go. The last assembly point is filled in for you.
  • Loud and hard to miss. The alert stays on the phone screen until it's tapped, and a red banner sits on every page of the app until the roll call is finished.
  • Tick people safe. Work down the list at the assembly point. Anyone without phone alerts is marked so you know who to look for.
  • Recorded. Every alert and roll call is kept with the time, the message and who was ticked safe.

Hours & timesheets

The timesheet writes itself.

Every sign-in and sign-out becomes hours, site by site, day by day. Check it, approve the week, send it to the office.

  • Your rules. Set start times, grace periods and how early people can sign in. Early arrivals are paid from the start time.
  • Missed sign-outs flagged. Anyone still signed in at midnight is signed out and flagged for the site manager to fix.
  • Changes shown, not hidden. Change a day's hours and it shows as changed, with what was clocked kept alongside.
  • Export for accounts. One tap gives a spreadsheet of the approved week. Pay and CIS columns are for admins only.
  • Operatives see their own hours. Fewer phone calls on a Friday.

Plant

Every machine on site, checked, photographed and signed for.

From the hire sheet to the off-hire collection, the plant record builds as you go.

Hire sheets and who has what

Upload the hire company's sheet (PDF, Excel or CSV) and see what's new and what's gone. Then give each machine to a person, with Change and Remove when it moves on. Search a name to see everything they've got.

On-hire and off-hire photos

Check and photograph a machine when it's delivered, and again when it's collected. One report puts them side by side, so you can deal with damage claims quickly.

Report an issue

Pick the machine, say what's wrong, add photos. It's emailed straight to your plant department, and a "stop using it" report flags the machine as do not use.

  • Daily pre-use checks with defects flagged to the site manager.
  • PUWER weekly inspections of every machine, signed off in one go.
  • LOLER certificates stored with expiry warnings for lifting equipment.
  • Search 100+ machines by fleet number, machine, hire firm or person.

Site paperwork

Everything an auditor asks for, one tap away.

Every register and record lives under one box on the site manager's home screen. Each one prints or saves as a PDF.

  • Registers: plant, PUWER weekly, LOLER and daily checks.
  • Inspections: H&S monthly and weekly forms, or your own.
  • Near misses reported from site and closed out by the manager.
  • HAVS vibration points logged by tool and minutes.
  • Site diary with notes, photos, weather and files.

Getting started

Up and running in four steps.

  1. Add your sites

    Stand on site and tap "Set sign-in point to where I'm standing". That's the sign-in area done.

  2. Add your people

    Each operative gets a set-up code. They enter it on their phone and take their set-up photo.

  3. Issue your RAMS

    Upload the PDF or type the briefing. It's signed at their next sign-in.

  4. Upload a hire sheet

    Your plant list builds itself and the on-hire checks are ready to do.
