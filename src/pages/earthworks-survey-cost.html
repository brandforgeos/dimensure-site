What drives the price

Site size and shape

Larger sites take longer to control and fly, and long, narrow sites need more control along their length for the same accuracy.

What the comparison is against

A 3D design surface from your engineer goes straight into the comparison. If the design is only on 2D levels drawings, we build the surface first, which takes office time.

How the volumes are split

One total for the site is quick. Splitting the quantities by roads, plots, open space or by phase, to match a bill of quantities, takes longer and is priced when we quote.

Ground the drone can't see

Hedges, trees and standing crops hide the ground. Those areas are levelled with the RTK GNSS rover so the volumes are complete.

How our prices compare

Published UK guides in 2026 put drone mapping of a 10-hectare development site at £1,500–£4,000 (Dronedesk's UK drone survey price guide, May 2026), and volume analysis is often an extra. Our prices are for the finished quantities, comparison included.
