Covering: all of Milton Keynes including the Western and Eastern Expansion Areas, plus Newport Pagnell, Olney, Wolverton, Stony Stratford, Bletchley and Woburn Sands. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

Built for the expansion areas

Milton Keynes' expansion areas, such as the Western Expansion Area at Whitehouse and Fairfields and the Eastern Expansion Area around Broughton and Brooklands, are big, long-running housing schemes. That means long programmes of estate roads, redways and parking courts, and plots reaching oversite every week.

On sites at that scale, a consistent check matters as much as an accurate one. We scan every plot at oversite the same way, with the same one-page report, and check each road layer against design before it's covered, leaving a dated record for the adoption engineer.

Homes and gardens across MK

MK's housing is younger than most towns, but plenty of 1970s and 80s homes in the older grid squares are now being extended and re-kitchened. A measured building survey gives designers exact existing drawings, and a garden scan captures levels, boundaries and drainage before a landscape design is drawn.

From plots to distribution sheds

Milton Keynes builds everything from single homes to large distribution units. A topographical survey is the starting point for designing on a plot; an as-built survey records each finished phase of road and drainage; and a floor flatness survey proves a warehouse or unit floor is within its tolerance before the racking goes in.
