Covering: Market Harborough, Lubenham, Great Bowden, Kibworth, Husbands Bosworth, Lutterworth and the villages of south Leicestershire. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

New neighbourhoods

Market Harborough's growth areas, and the new estates around Kibworth and Lutterworth, mean estate roads and plots being built for years to come. We check road layers and plots at oversite against design, record finished phases in as-built surveys, and run pre-adoption road surveys so defects are fixed before Leicestershire's highway inspection. Before anything is built, a Design Audit finds the drawing errors that cause problems later.

The town centre and older streets

The historic centre has a conservation area and plenty of listed buildings, with Victorian and Edwardian streets around it. A measured building survey gives architects exact existing drawings, and for listed buildings our listed building surveys record the building without touching it. Shops with flats above are a common fit for a lease plan survey.

Homes, gardens and moving buildings

For extensions and garden designs, garden and topographical surveys give the levels. Where a floor slopes or a wall bulges, floor level and wall verticality surveys measure exactly how far.

The villages

South Leicestershire's villages have barns, stables and riding arenas aplenty: barn conversion surveys and arena level surveys cover them.
