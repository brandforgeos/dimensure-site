What decides the price

Rooms, not floor area

Each room needs its own scanner set-up, so a house of small rooms takes longer than an open-plan floor of the same size.

How clear the floors are

Clearing rugs and moving furniture off the main floor areas before we arrive gives a better result in less time.

What you need back

A contour plan of the floors is the core of the survey. Full measured plans, elevations or levels outside the house add drawing time.

Included

  • Level contour plan over the floor plan
  • Spot levels on a grid and at doors
  • High and low points of each floor
  • PDF and DWG
  • Registered point cloud
