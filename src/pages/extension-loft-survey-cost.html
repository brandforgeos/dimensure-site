What decides the price

How much of the house

A single-storey rear extension needs the back of the house. A loft conversion needs the top floor, the stair and the roof. A wrap-around or two-storey extension needs more.

Size and age

Larger and older houses, with more rooms and fewer straight walls, take more set-ups and more drawing time.

Loft access

A boarded, lit loft with a ladder is quick. A loft full of stored boxes hides the structure we need to see.
