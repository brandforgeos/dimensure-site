Covering: Stony Stratford, Wolverton, Old Wolverton, Calverton, Fairfields, Whitehouse and the surrounding villages of north-west Milton Keynes. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

Stony Stratford: old buildings, careful work

Stony Stratford's High Street is lined with Georgian fronts, timber-framed buildings behind them and long burgage plots running back from the road, much of it in a conservation area and plenty of it listed. Those are buildings where the drawings rarely match reality. A listed building measured survey records walls, floors and roof lines as they actually are, without fixing anything to the fabric, and a lease plan survey sorts out the shops and flats that have been divided up over the years.

Wolverton: railway terraces and conversions

Wolverton grew up around the railway works, and its long streets of Victorian terraces are now being extended into, lofted and split into flats. For architects, a measured building survey gives exact existing plans and elevations; for owners worried about a sloping floor or a cracked party wall, a floor level survey and a wall verticality survey give the engineer measured evidence to work from.

Fairfields and Whitehouse: the new estates

To the south, the Western Expansion Area is still building out. On the construction side we check road layers and plots at oversite against design, record finished phases in as-built surveys, and run pre-adoption road surveys so defects are fixed before the highway authority's inspection.

Gardens and plots

For new garden designs, extensions and self-builds, a garden survey or topographical survey gives levels, boundaries and trees, with the full 3D scan for the designer.
