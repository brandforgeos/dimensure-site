What decides the price

How much can be seen

A large opening into a clear vault is quick to scan. A small hole into a long, partly filled vault needs more set-ups and more care to get the scanner's view where it's needed.

What the engineer needs

A point cloud alone is the quickest output. Drawn plans and sections relating the vault to the neighbouring wall, boundary and piles take office time, so they're priced on what's needed.

Distance and urgency

Within 50 miles of Corby, travel is included. Further away, travel is added and shown on the quote.
