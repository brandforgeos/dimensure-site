What decides the price

Length and height

A short garden wall needs a couple of scanner positions. A long boundary or a three-storey gable needs more, from further back, to see the whole face.

Both sides or one

Scanning both faces shows the thickness and the full shape of the wall, but needs access to both sides, sometimes through a neighbour's property.

Vegetation

Ivy and shrubs hide the wall face. Cutting back growth before we arrive, where that's safe and allowed, gives a more complete survey.
