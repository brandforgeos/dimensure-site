What decides the price

Area

Open surfaces are quick to scan, so price rises slowly with size. A full-size pitch takes longer than an arena, but not ten times longer.

One visit or two

A single survey shows the surface. Measuring depth means surveying the base first and the surface after, which is two visits.

What you want back

A levels and high/low plan is standard. Cut-and-fill volumes to a new design level, or a regrade plan for a contractor, add office time.

Included

  • Laser scan and GNSS survey
  • Levels and contour plan
  • High and low spot plan
  • Falls checked
  • Registered point cloud
