Inspect

Every stage of every plot has its own sheet: the checks, the measurements, before, during and completed photos, delivery tickets and drawing references.

Stage sheets

Fourteen stages, from foundation dig to handover

Each stage has a sheet built for that job, so the right questions get asked every time on every plot.

  • Yes, No or N/A on every check, with measurements and notes where they matter.
  • Before, during and completed photos, three at a time, straight from the camera.
  • Concrete and material delivery tickets photographed on the sheet.
  • Drawing references and revisions cited, so you know what it was built to.
  • Signed on the screen by the team and the site manager.
Site manager

Your day on one screen

Open the app and the work is waiting: sheets to sign off, overdue defects, variations at risk and advisories for your house types.

  • Sheets to sign off listed by plot, stage and who filled them in.
  • NHBC stage scores recorded on the sheet, with failures turned into defects.
  • Stage order enforced, with a reasoned override when the site needs one.
  • Every plot's progress at a glance, from first dig to CML.
Defects

Defects found, fixed and closed, with the proof

A defect raised on a sheet stays open until a fix sheet closes it, with photos and a sign-off.

  • Open, overdue and due-soon defects up front, by plot and stage.
  • Cause recorded: workmanship, design or materials. Only workmanship counts against a team's score.
  • Fix attempts logged with the remedial work and a re-score.
  • Technical advisories pushed to every plot of the same house type.
Variations

Every variation tracked from site to order

Raised on site with photos and drawings, priced by the QS, sent to the principal contractor and chased until the order comes back.

  • Secured and not-yet-ordered values at the top of the register.
  • Commercial risk flagged when work is done without an order.
  • Hold-ups listed, so everyone knows what's stopping it going to the PC.
  • Values seen only by the QS, Contracts Manager and Admin.
