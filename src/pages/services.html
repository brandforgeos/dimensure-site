One scanner, one standard

Every job we scan is scanned with a Trimble X9, a survey-grade terrestrial laser scanner that levels and calibrates itself at each set-up. The manufacturer specifies 2 mm range accuracy and 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m.

Every report states the registration error for that job, which is how well the individual scans tie together. If the data isn't tight enough for the check you need, we tell you rather than hand over a number we wouldn't stand behind. The same check applies when you send us your own survey data to report on.
