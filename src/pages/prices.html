What affects the price of a laser scan survey

Time on site

What decides the time on site is how many scanner set-ups the job needs. A straight road needs fewer than one with bends and turning heads; an open-plan room fewer than a house of small rooms; an open lawn fewer than a garden full of walls and trees.

What you need back

A pass/fail QC report built from your design is quicker to produce than full drawn plans and elevations. A point cloud on its own is the cheapest deliverable; detailed CAD drawing takes the most office time.

The quality of the information you send

A 3D design surface or a clean DWG with levels goes straight into the comparison. Scanned PDFs mean we build the design first. It's still fine, but it takes longer.

Batching

The biggest saving. Scanning six plots on one visit costs far less per plot than six separate trips, and a road and its plots on the same day share the travel and set-up.

Deadline and access

Most work is booked within a few days. Same-day or out-of-hours scans, and sites with restricted access or escort requirements, can cost more.
