Guide prices before VAT. A condition survey is a factual record of visible condition, not a structural survey or a party wall award.

What drives the price

Extent

The length of road and footpath, and the number of properties, decide how long the record takes on site and how long the defect schedule takes to write.

Interiors

Recording inside a neighbouring house takes longer, needs an appointment with the owner and adds to the schedule, so it's priced per property.

Before and after

The post-completion survey reuses the same extent, control and method, which keeps it the same price and makes the comparison like for like.

How our prices compare

Published guidance puts a schedule of condition from around £450 plus VAT at 2020 prices, with typical party wall schedules estimated at £1,500–£2,000 (Designing Buildings Wiki, party wall surveyor costs, checked October 2026). Ours start below that, and include a laser scan most photographic schedules don't have.

Included on every condition survey

  • Laser scan of roads, kerbs and walls
  • Dated drone aerial of the frontage
  • Photos referenced to a location plan
  • Written schedule of existing defects
  • Data kept for at least six years
  • Report within 3 working days
