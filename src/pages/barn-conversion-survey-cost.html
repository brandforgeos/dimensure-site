What decides the price

Size and storeys

A single open barn is quicker than a two-storey barn with lofts, stalls and lean-tos, each of which needs its own scanner positions.

How irregular it is

Drawing a leaning rubble wall or a twisted roof honestly takes longer than drawing a straight one. That's the point of the survey, so we price it in rather than simplify it out.

Access and clutter

Barns full of machinery, bales or stored materials hide the walls and floor. Clearing what you can before we arrive saves time and gives a more complete survey.
