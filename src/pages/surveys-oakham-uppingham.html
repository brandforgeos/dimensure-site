Covering: Oakham, Barleythorpe, Uppingham, Langham, Cottesmore, Lyddington and the villages between. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included. For the rest of the county, see surveys in Rutland.

Old town centres

Oakham and Uppingham both have conservation areas full of stone buildings, many listed, from town houses to former school and coaching buildings. Altering them starts with accurate existing drawings. Our listed building surveys scan inside and out without fixing anything to the fabric, and show the real wall thicknesses, floor levels and roof lines. Where a building has been split into shops and flats, a lease plan survey gives each part a proper scaled plan.

Older houses that have moved

Centuries-old houses often have floors that slope and walls that lean, and most of the time that's just age. When it's getting worse, or a buyer's surveyor has flagged it, a floor level survey and a wall verticality survey give the structural engineer measured evidence, and a repeat survey shows whether anything is still moving.

Newer estates around Oakham

Barleythorpe and the newer estates on the edge of Oakham are full of homes that are now being extended, converted and re-landscaped. A measured building survey and a garden survey give the designer everything they need without guesswork.

Barns, stables and arenas

Around both towns, farm buildings are being converted and yards keep horses. We provide barn conversion surveys, topographical surveys of the land around them, and arena level surveys for riding arenas and manèges.
