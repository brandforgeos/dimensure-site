Covering: Newport Pagnell, Olney, Sherington, Emberton, Lavendon, Hanslope and the villages of north Buckinghamshire. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

Olney: limestone cottages and conversions

Olney's market place and side streets are built in local limestone, with cottages, coach houses and outbuildings that have been altered for centuries. Thick, uneven walls and low ceilings make them hard to measure by hand. A listed building and barn conversion survey scans the whole building and draws it as it stands, and a point cloud survey suits architects who'd rather draw it themselves.

Newport Pagnell: High Street to the new estates

Newport Pagnell's High Street has shops with flats above, many split and re-let over the years; a lease plan survey gives each one a proper scaled plan with its floor area. Around the town, newer estates are being extended and re-landscaped, which is where measured building surveys and garden surveys come in.

Riverside gardens and sloping plots

Gardens that run down towards the Ouse and the Ouzel are rarely flat. A topographical survey or garden survey records the levels, walls, steps and trees so a designer or builder can plan patios, drainage and retaining walls properly. Where an old boundary or retaining wall has started to lean, a wall verticality survey measures exactly how far.

Villages and farms

In the villages around, we survey barns for conversion, riding arenas and manèges, and plots for self-builds and small developments.
