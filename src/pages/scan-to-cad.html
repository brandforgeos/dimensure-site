In short: scan to CAD is a drawing service. You send a point cloud from any laser scanner (E57, RCP/RCS, LAS or LAZ) and we draw it as 2D CAD: floor plans, elevations, sections or a topographical plan, in DWG and PDF. We check the data before drawing, there's no site visit, and it's available anywhere in the UK. Quoted per job once we've seen the data.

DWG and PDF, to your template if you have one

None, UK-wide

What we draw

  • Floor plans with walls, openings, stairs, room heights and levels.
  • Elevations of buildings and interior walls.
  • Sections through buildings, roofs, stairwells or structures.
  • Topographical plans: levels, contours, kerbs, boundaries and features.
  • Specific elements: a facade, a steel frame, a vault or a run of pipework.

We check the data first

A drawing is only as good as the scan behind it. Before we draw, we look at registration quality, coverage and noise, and tell you if there are gaps or errors that will affect the result. If it's fine, we draw. If it isn't, you hear about it before you pay for drawings built on bad data.

Who uses scan to CAD

  • Firms with their own scanner and no drafting time
  • Architects sent a scan by a client
  • Contractors with scan data from site
  • Surveyors needing overflow drafting

Don't have a scan yet?

We can do that too. A point cloud survey gives you the registered scan, and a measured building survey or topographical survey includes the drawings as well.

How to send your data

Email info@dimensure.co.uk with a download link to the point cloud, a note of what you need drawn and any CAD template. We'll reply with a data check and a fixed price.
