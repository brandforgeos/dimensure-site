Covering: Wellingborough, Rushden, Higham Ferrers, Irthlingborough, Raunds, Finedon, Earls Barton and the Nene valley villages. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

The new estates

Wellingborough is home to some of Northamptonshire's largest housing schemes, including Stanton Cross beside the station. Long build programmes mean miles of estate road and hundreds of plots, and consistent checks pay for themselves. We scan sub-base and binder against design, measure tarmac thickness, check every plot at oversite, and run pre-adoption road surveys so ponding and low kerbs are fixed before the highway authority's inspection. A Design Audit finds drawing errors before any of it is built.

Warehouses and industrial units

Along the A45 and A14, new distribution and industrial units need their floors proven before racking goes in. A floor flatness survey scans the whole slab, and our guide to SR1, SR2 and SR3 explains the classes. Lease plans and floor areas follow when the units are let.

Terraces and converted factories

Rushden, Higham Ferrers and Wellingborough grew on boot and shoe making, and many of the old factories are now flats. That means lease plans, measured surveys of the existing building, and floor level surveys where old floors have moved. The streets of terraced houses around them are being extended and lofted, which starts with a measured survey too.

Gardens and villages

For homes in the villages and on the edge of town, garden surveys and topographical surveys give designers the levels and boundaries to work from.
