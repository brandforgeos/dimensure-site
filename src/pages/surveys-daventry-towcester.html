Covering: Daventry, Towcester, Brackley, Weedon Bec, Long Buckby, Roade and the villages of south and west Northamptonshire. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

Warehouses and logistics

The M1 corridor around Daventry is one of the country's busiest spots for distribution warehouses. Big slabs need proving before the racking arrives: a floor flatness survey scans the whole floor against its class, and our guide to SR1, SR2 and SR3 explains what those classes mean. When units are let, lease plans and floor areas follow.

New estates around the towns

Daventry, Towcester and Brackley have all grown with new housing. On site, we check road layers and plots at oversite against design and run pre-adoption road surveys before the highway inspection. Before the build, a Design Audit catches drawing errors on paper.

Market towns and stone villages

Towcester's Watling Street frontage and the ironstone villages around it are full of older buildings. Barn conversion and listed building surveys draw them as they really are, and extension and loft surveys give architects what they need for the newer homes.

Land, gardens and arenas

For plots, paddocks and gardens, topographical and garden surveys record the levels, and arena level surveys serve the area's yards and equestrian centres.
