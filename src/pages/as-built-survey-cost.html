What drives the price

How much there is to record

A straight road with a few gullies is quicker than a network of roads, private drives and parking courts with dozens of covers. We price from your layout, so you know the cost before we book.

Drawing requirements

A clean as-built in our standard layers is included. If your engineer or the adopting authority needs a particular CAD standard, title block or layer naming, tell us and we'll include it in the price.

Doing it alongside the QC surveys

The cheapest as-built is the one drawn from a scan we already took. If we're checking the surface course or tarmac depth on a phase, the as-built can come from the same data.

Included on every as-built survey

  • As-built drawing in DWG and PDF
  • Levels, kerb lines and cover positions
  • Cover levels on every visible chamber
  • Tied to your site control
  • Registered point cloud
  • Drawings typically in 5 working days
