Covering: Bedford, Kempston, Wixams, Biddenham, Cranfield, Sharnbrook and the villages of Bedfordshire. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

New settlements and estates

South of Bedford, Wixams and the other growth areas are building whole new neighbourhoods. On sites like these we check sub-base and binder before each layer is covered, measure tarmac thickness, check plots at oversite, and record sewers and ducts as laid before backfill. When the roads are finished, a pre-adoption road survey finds the ponding, low kerbs and proud ironwork before the highway authority does.

Victorian Bedford

The streets of Victorian and Edwardian houses around the town centre and the river are being extended, lofted and split into flats. That calls for measured building surveys, lease plans for each flat, and, where floors and walls have moved, floor level and wall verticality surveys for the engineer.

Industrial and logistics

New units around Bedford and Kempston need their floors proven before racking goes in. A floor flatness survey scans the whole slab against its class, and floor areas and lease plans follow when units are let.

Land and gardens

For new plots, self-builds and garden designs, a topographical survey or garden survey gives levels, boundaries and trees, with the 3D scan included.
