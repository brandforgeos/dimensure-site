Covering: St Neots, Eynesbury, Eaton Socon, Huntingdon, Godmanchester, St Ives and the villages along the A1 and A14 in Cambridgeshire. Every Dimensure service is available, with travel included.

New neighbourhoods

St Neots and Huntingdon both have large new developments on their edges, with years of estate roads and plots still to build. We check sub-base and binder against design, measure tarmac thickness, check plots at oversite, record drainage as laid, and run pre-adoption road surveys before the highway authority's inspection.

Riverside towns and older homes

The older streets near the Great Ouse are full of houses being extended, lofted and modernised. Extension and loft surveys give architects accurate existing drawings, and kitchen surveys let designers order units that fit. Where floors slope or walls lean, floor level and wall verticality surveys give the engineer measured evidence.

Shops, offices and units

Town-centre premises and units along the A14 need lease plans and floor areas when they're let, and new industrial floors need a floor flatness survey before racking goes in.

Land and gardens

For plots, gardens and small developments, topographical and garden surveys give levels, boundaries and trees with the full 3D scan.
