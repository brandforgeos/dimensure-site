What drives the price

Area and layout

One open warehouse floor is quick to scan. The same area split into offices, corridors and plant rooms needs far more scanner positions.

What's on the floor

The scanner measures what it can see. Pallets, materials or access equipment on the slab hide the surface behind them, so a clear floor costs less and gives a complete result.

Phasing

Surveying each bay as it's poured catches problems while the floor contractor is still working. It means more visits, which we price as one programme rather than separate call-outs.
