What decides the price

Rooms and stairs

Each room and stair needs its own scanner set-ups. One kitchen is quick; a kitchen, utility, two bedrooms and a staircase on the same visit costs more in total but less per room.

What you want drawn

A plan and wall elevations are standard. Extra details, such as large-scale drawings of a bay window or a chimney breast, add drawing time.

Clutter

Clear worktops and walls mean a complete survey in less time.
