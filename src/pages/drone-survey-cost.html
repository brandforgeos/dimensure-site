What drives the price

Area and shape

Flight time and the number of photos grow with area. A compact square site is quicker to fly and control than a long, thin corridor of the same area, which needs more control points along its length.

Accuracy needed

A site plan for an appraisal needs less control than a surface you'll calculate earthworks from. More control and check points mean more time on the ground with the rover, so we agree the accuracy before we quote.

Ground cover

Open ground is quick. Woodland, tall crops and dense hedges hide the ground from the camera, so those areas are levelled with the rover or scanned instead.

What you need drawn

The orthophoto, surface model and contours are standard. Fully drawn features such as kerbs, fences, buildings and covers take more office time and are priced when we quote.

How our prices compare

Published UK guides in 2026 put a small-site drone mapping survey at around £700–£2,500 (Dronedesk's UK drone survey price guide, May 2026), and some providers start drone topographic surveys from £599 for half a hectare (SkyScan Surveys' published pricing, checked October 2026). Our prices sit at or below the lower end and include RTK ground control and Ordnance Survey grid as standard.
